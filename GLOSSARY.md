# Zaira Furnishing Domain Model

The core bespoke luxury home furnishing catalog, consultation, tailoring, and order management platform.

## Language

**Catalog**:
The curated collection of bespoke furnishing categories, subcategories, products, and materials offered by the atelier.
_Avoid_: Inventory, item list, shop

**Category**:
A primary architectural furnishing discipline (e.g., Curtains & Drapes, Window Blinds, Sofa Fabrics, Wallpapers, Carpets).
_Avoid_: Department, section, group

**Subcategory**:
A specific styling or functional classification within a Category (e.g., Sheer Curtains, Roller Blinds, Boucle Upholstery).
_Avoid_: Type, subtype, classification

**Product**:
An individual furnishing item available in the catalog, either standard or custom-made, with distinct materials, variations, and specifications.
_Avoid_: SKU, article, item, listing

**Variation**:
A material, finish, color, weave, or pattern option for a Product.
_Avoid_: Variant option, sub-item, property
